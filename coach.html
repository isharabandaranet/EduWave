Coach

The upgrade you need to maximize your Z-Score.

What is

EduWave Coach

EduWave Coach is your personal digital tutor. While 'Learn' lets you practice, 'Coach' tells you how to improve. We use data from your answers to show you exactly which topics you are struggling with and provide detailed, step-by-step solutions to fix those gaps.

Unlock Your Full Potential

Replace static books with interactive, timed quizzes that simulate the real exam environment.

01

Pinpoint Weaknesses

Stop losing marks blindly. Our system identifies exactly which topics need more attention so you can study efficiently.

02

Time Practice

Train your brain to work faster. Our built-in countdown timer ensures you learn to manage your time effectively.

03

Learn the Logic

Don't just memorize. Access detailed, step-by-step reasoning for every answer to understand the theory behind it.

04

National Ranking

See your true standing. Compare your marks against thousands of other A/L students across the island.

05

Visual Progress

Watch your Z-score grow. Our dashboard turns your practice data into easy-to-read graphs and trend lines.

06

Mistake Manager

Focus on what matters. We automatically save your wrong answers so you can revisit and master them later.
